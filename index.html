01 About the hackathon

Built for Bangladesh's AI generation.

The first national-level AI hackathon in Bangladesh that uses Z.ai's GLM model family as its core platform. Organized by Rudra Sarker, Z.ai Ambassador for Bangladesh, together with RoboSUST Club and partner universities across the country.

🎯

Purpose

Give every Bangladeshi student — not just CSE majors — a structured first experience building real AI products with frontier models, not synthetic demos.

🌐

National scope

Open to students across Bangladesh. Virtual qualifiers feed into an in-person final at SUST, Sylhet — with partner hubs in Dhaka, Chattogram, and Rajshahi.

⚡

Why Z.ai

GLM-5.2 — with its 1M-token context window, agentic tools (AutoGLM, AutoClaw), and the ZCode IDE — is the right platform for a country where access to frontier AI has historically been the bottleneck.

02 Why Bangladesh · Why now

A developer ecosystem ready to move.

Bangladesh has one of the largest student developer populations in South Asia. What it has not had — until now — is a national AI event anchored on a single frontier platform with real ambassador-led support on the ground.

04 Participation

Who can join.

→

Eligibility

  • Currently enrolled student at any Bangladeshi university (public or private).
  • All departments welcome — CSE, SWE, EEE, IPE, business, design, liberal arts.
  • Beginner-friendly: no prior AI experience required.
  • International students studying in Bangladesh are welcome.
→

Team size

  • Preferred: 2–4 members.
  • Solo entries allowed with organizer approval.
  • One participant can be on only one team.
  • Cross-university teams encouraged.

How to apply

  1. Fill the registration form (link in Register section).
  2. Receive your team ID and Z.ai API starter pack.

  4. Submit your project by the deadline (see Timeline).

What you get

  • Free Z.ai API credits for the event.
  • Python and web starter kits pre-wired to GLM.
  • Certificate of participation for every valid submission.
  • Mentorship from Z.ai ambassadors and SUST faculty.

09 FAQ

Common questions.

QDo I need to know AI or ML before joining?

No. The hackathon is beginner-friendly. We provide starter kits for Python and web that are pre-wired to Z.ai's GLM API, and we run a virtual onboarding workshop before the build window opens.

QIs there a registration fee?

No. Registration is completely free for all enrolled students. Z.ai API credits during the event window are also provided at no cost to participants.

QCan I participate from outside SUST?

Yes. The qualifier is fully online. The in-person final is hosted at SUST, Sylhet, but we are working with partner universities to set up satellite viewing hubs in Dhaka, Chattogram, and Rajshahi.

QWhat if I don't have a team?

You can register solo and we'll help match you with teammates through the official Discord tournament channel. Cross-university teams are encouraged.

QHow is Z.ai involved?

Z.ai is the title supporter of the event. The entire hackathon is built on the GLM model family (GLM-5, GLM-5.1, GLM-5 Turbo, GLM-5V Turbo, GLM-5.2) and on Z.ai's agentic tools (AutoGLM, AutoClaw) and ZCode IDE.

QWill my project need to be open-sourced?

Open-sourcing is encouraged and earns bonus weight on the judging rubric, but is not strictly required to participate. Winning teams are expected to publish a short technical write-up.

QWhat are the prizes?

Cash prizes, certificates of achievement, mentorship opportunities, and featured showcase on Z.ai and ambassador channels. Final prize pool will be announced with the official rules document.

QHow can my university get involved as a partner?

Email or DM the ambassador (see contact). We onboard partner universities as satellite hubs, judging partners, or co-organizers depending on their capacity.
